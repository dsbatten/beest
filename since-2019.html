Bitcoin Growth vs Emissions

I set hashrate (7-day average), net emissions, total emissions and BTC price to 100 on 1 September 2019, the first day of the series, so you can see how far each has moved. A value of 200 means double its level on that day.

  • Reading the chartI use a log scale, so equal distances up the chart are equal percentage changes. The gap between hashrate and emissions is the security the network added without adding emissions to match. As I read it, that gap comes from more efficient machines, a cleaner energy mix and, for net emissions, methane destruction.
  • What the hover showsWhen you hover over the chart, each series shows how far it has grown since 1 September 2019 relative to net emissions, so net emissions read 1.00×. A hashrate reading of 10.88× means hashrate has grown 10.88 times as much as net emissions over that period. The actual value sits beside it.
