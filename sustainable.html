Bitcoin Mining: Usage of Sustainable Energy

author: @dsbatten | source: BEEST 2 | data: Cambridge CCAF, BEEST 1, Ember, Coin Metrics | updated: –
Sustainable energy follows the Cambridge Centre for Alternative Finance definition: renewables (hydro, wind, solar and other) plus nuclear. Cambridge's 59.4% (December 2025) is a snapshot; from then on, the grid-connected share of mining (91.9%) moves with the world grid's clean share, which has risen by about one percentage point a year since 2019 (Ember).
